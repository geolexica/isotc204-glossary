# ISO/TC 204 ITS Vocabulary — Edition 1 (2022)

## Publication

Originally published as **ISO/TS 14812:2022** — *Intelligent transport systems — Vocabulary*.
This was the first formal vocabulary standard from ISO/TC 204, issued as a
Technical Specification (the "TS" prefix).

[Order from ISO](https://www.iso.org/standard/79779.html)

## Scope

This edition defines ~320 concepts spanning the full ITS domain — service
domains, transport entities, data and communications concepts, system
architecture viewpoints, and performance management.

## Source

The data in this dataset was originally imported from the **ISO/TC 204
Multi-Lingual Glossary of Terms (MLGT)** spreadsheet maintained by the
ISO/TC 204 terminology working group. It was converted to the Glossarist
v3 YAML format for machine-readable consumption.

## Status

**Superseded** by Edition 2 (ISO 14812:2025). This dataset is kept for
historical reference and to anchor the lineage-series timeline — every
concept that existed in 2022 has a forward `supersedes` edge to its
successor in Edition 2 (where the concept persisted) or is marked
withdrawn (where Edition 2 dropped it).
