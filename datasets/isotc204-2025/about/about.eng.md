# ISO/TC 204 ITS Vocabulary — Edition 2 (2025)

## Publication

Published as **ISO 14812:2025** — *Intelligent transport systems — Vocabulary*.
With this edition, the document graduated from Technical Specification
to a full International Standard (the "TS" prefix was dropped).

## Scope

~310 concepts. Includes revisions to ~300 concepts carried over from
Edition 1, plus a small number of new concepts and ~9 withdrawals of
concepts that did not survive the ballot process.

## Source

The data in this dataset is generated by converting the Enterprise
Architect XMI export of the ISO/TC 204 vocabulary model — the same source
used to produce the printed standard. The conversion is performed by the
`Iso14812Import` converter under `lib/iso14812_import/` in this repo.

## Status

**Superseded** by Edition 3 (draft). This dataset is kept as the
current balloted publication; Edition 3 is the working draft that
will eventually replace it. Every concept in this edition has a forward
`supersedes` edge to its Edition 3 successor (where preserved) or is
marked withdrawn.
